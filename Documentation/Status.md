## Where things stand

- **2.0.0** is tagged and pushed (12 Nov 2026). `configure()` takes no values: the relay URL is in the
  obfuscated `Secrets.swift`, and the token comes from the host's Info.plist key `ClarityFeedbackAppToken`.
  Redirects are refused, and users only ever see three fixed messages.
- 20 tests in 3 suites pass with `swift test`, and SwiftLint is clean (17 Nov 2026).
- The test target links `ClarityTesting` from ClarityLinks (17 Nov 2026), and the five tests written for
  #255, #679 and #680 carry `.clarity(…)` traits.
- The repo is public; ClarityLinks is private.
